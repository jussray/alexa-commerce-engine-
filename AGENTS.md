# Founder Intelligence continuity contract

Founder Control Room is the upstream continuity/governance source for this repository. It does not override stronger local commerce, money-truth, safety, evidence, or authorization constraints.

## TRUE-FIRST

Before classifying a claim as false, establish the strongest evidence-bound TRUE baseline available for the exact subject and scope. Bind that baseline to the exact repository, branch, immutable head/version, scope, and evidence set. A continuity fingerprint or cookie is `EVIDENCE_ONLY`: it carries provenance and freshness, never authority. If the bound head/version, scope, evidence, or freshness moves, the prior cookie becomes stale and must not be reused as current proof. `STALE != FALSE`.

Run the Founder Intelligence challenge stack in this exact order:

1. ULTRATHINK
2. Red Team 1 — premise
3. Lindy mode
4. L99
5. Red Team 2 — implementation
6. OODA
7. Proof
8. Rollback / Next Gate

Proof means current evidence for the exact head and path being claimed. For UI/runtime claims, Playwright evidence is required before calling the path verified.

Fingerprints, cookies, receipts, challenge-stack completion, CI, and Playwright are evidence only. They do not grant standing merge, deploy, publication, provider-write, spending, deletion, purchase, or other consequential authority.

For Shopping Compass, a recommendation, receipt, prepared handoff, browser result, or challenge-stack pass cannot authorize a purchase, spending, Amazon action, provider mutation, deployment/publication, or a claim of commission/customer outcome. Existing money-truth boundaries remain binding.
